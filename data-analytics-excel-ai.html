What you will learn and use

Tools & working environment

Microsoft Excel (or compatible spreadsheet software), sample CSV datasets, charts, PivotTables and an AI assistant.

How the learning works

  • Learn the concept through guided instruction and examples.
  • Complete two practical assignments each week.
  • Submit your work for review and improve it using feedback.
  • Keep your project files and notes organised as a portfolio.

What you should have by the end

  • Track-specific practical files and task submissions.
  • One completed final project with documentation.
  • Evidence of your work such as screenshots, code, workbook or report.
  • Experience explaining your approach in a project demonstration.
  • A clearer view of the next skills to learn for entry-level roles.
WEEK-BY-WEEK PLAN

Your complete 6-week roadmap

Each week includes two practical assignments. Exact task sheets, submission dates, mentor review schedule and delivery mode will be shared during onboarding.

WEEK 01Spreadsheet and data basics

Week 1 — Spreadsheet and data basics

What you will learn

  • Understand workbook, worksheets, rows, columns, tables and data types.
  • Format raw data, apply number/date formats and create structured tables.

Practical assignments

  • Clean a messy student/sales dataset.
  • Identify blanks, duplicates, inconsistent dates and incorrect entries.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

WEEK 02Formulas and functions

Week 2 — Formulas and functions

What you will learn

  • Practise SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, COUNTA and IF.
  • Use SUMIF, COUNTIF and lookup concepts for practical reporting.

Practical assignments

  • Create an automated marks/payroll summary sheet.
  • Build formula-based category-wise totals and checks.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

WEEK 03Analysis and PivotTables

Week 3 — Analysis and PivotTables

What you will learn

  • Sort, filter and use conditional formatting.
  • Summarise data with PivotTables and charts.

Practical assignments

  • Analyse monthly sales or attendance data.
  • Create PivotTable summaries and three meaningful charts.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

WEEK 04AI-assisted analysis

Week 4 — AI-assisted analysis

What you will learn

  • Write prompts to explain formulas and suggest analysis steps.
  • Verify AI-generated formulas, assumptions and outputs against sample data.

Practical assignments

  • Use AI to draft formula explanations and data insights.
  • Prepare an error-checking log showing what you verified or corrected.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

WEEK 05Dashboard project

Week 5 — Dashboard project

What you will learn

  • Choose KPIs, design layout and add charts/slicers where supported.
  • Build a dashboard with clear titles, filters and summary cards.

Practical assignments

  • Create a sales/attendance dashboard.
  • Test filters, formulas and totals against the source data.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

WEEK 06Reporting and presentation

Week 6 — Reporting and presentation

What you will learn

  • Write a concise findings report with limitations and recommendations.
  • Prepare a walkthrough explaining data, formulas and dashboard choices.

Practical assignments

  • Submit workbook, source dataset and PDF/report export.
  • Present dashboard and answer assessment questions.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

CAPSTONE • FINAL SUBMISSION

Your final project

A Sales or Attendance Analytics Dashboard with cleaned source data, formulas, PivotTable summaries, charts, key metrics and a short insight report.

Final deliverables: Project files, screenshots/demo evidence, short documentation or README, and an individual presentation/viva-style assessment.

CAREER PATHWAYS

What jobs can this skill lead towards?

This internship helps you build beginner-level exposure and a project to discuss in interviews. Job titles below are possible career directions—not a promise that this short internship alone qualifies you for each role.

Possible entry-level roles

  • MIS / Reporting Executive Trainee
  • Data Analyst Intern (entry-level)
  • Excel / Operations Analyst Assistant
  • Business Reporting / Data Operations Associate

Where to explore opportunities

Explore analytics consultancies, banks/financial operations, retail, logistics, BPO/KPO, SaaS and IT service companies. Role requirements vary; advanced analyst roles need further SQL, statistics and BI practice.

What to learn next

  • SQL fundamentals and data querying
  • Power BI or another BI tool
  • Statistics, data storytelling and Python for data analysis

Important: Sharing an opportunity is not a referral, interview call or placement guarantee. Always apply through the employer's official channel and verify the listing.

ASSESSMENT & CERTIFICATE

How completion is evaluated

Assessment components

  • Practical task completion and quality.
  • Final project functionality, relevance and originality.
  • Documentation and submission discipline.
  • Ability to explain your work and answer questions.

Certificate eligibility

Certificate is assessment-based. Students who complete the required activities, submit the final project and pass the assessment may qualify, subject to the programme's final review. Application or attendance alone does not guarantee a certificate.

Career transparency: This is a learning internship, not a guaranteed placement programme. Employer names mentioned as examples are not claimed as partners. Job openings change frequently and students are responsible for checking current requirements.

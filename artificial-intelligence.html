WEEK-BY-WEEK PLAN

Your complete 6-week roadmap

Each week includes two practical assignments. Exact task sheets, submission dates, mentor review schedule and delivery mode will be shared during onboarding.

WEEK 01AI foundations

Week 1 — AI foundations

What you will learn

  • Understand AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI at a beginner level.
  • Discuss examples, limitations, bias, privacy and responsible use.

Practical assignments

  • Create an AI use-case map for education or office work.
  • Compare AI and non-AI workflows for one simple task.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

WEEK 02Prompt engineering

Week 2 — Prompt engineering

What you will learn

  • Practise role, context, task, constraints and output format in prompts.
  • Iterate prompts and evaluate relevance, clarity and accuracy.

Practical assignments

  • Create a prompt library for five useful tasks.
  • Compare weak and improved prompts with documented results.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

WEEK 03Verification and productivity

Week 3 — Verification and productivity

What you will learn

  • Check facts, calculations, citations and assumptions in AI outputs.
  • Use AI to assist with summarisation, drafting or spreadsheet formulas.

Practical assignments

  • Prepare a verified summary from supplied material.
  • Create a human-review checklist for AI-generated work.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

WEEK 04Data and simple prototype

Week 4 — Data and simple prototype

What you will learn

  • Understand how examples/data influence an AI task.
  • Design a simple FAQ assistant or study-helper workflow using available tools.

Practical assignments

  • Prepare a small approved FAQ knowledge set.
  • Build a clickable/structured prototype or documented workflow.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

WEEK 05Test and improve

Week 5 — Test and improve

What you will learn

  • Test the prototype with normal, unclear and out-of-scope questions.
  • Record errors, hallucinations, privacy risks and improvement ideas.

Practical assignments

  • Create a test set with expected answers.
  • Improve prompts/flow and document limitations.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

WEEK 06Final demo

Week 6 — Final demo

What you will learn

  • Prepare a clear use-case, workflow diagram and user guide.
  • Explain where human review is required and what the prototype cannot do.

Practical assignments

  • Submit prototype/workflow, prompt set and test report.
  • Demonstrate it and answer assessment questions.

Weekly submission: Complete both practical tasks and submit files/screenshots/notes as instructed.

CAPSTONE • FINAL SUBMISSION

Your final project

An AI Study Helper or FAQ Assistant prototype with a curated sample knowledge set, tested prompts, documented limitations and human-review guidance.

Final deliverables: Project files, screenshots/demo evidence, short documentation or README, and an individual presentation/viva-style assessment.

ASSESSMENT & CERTIFICATE

How completion is evaluated

Assessment components

  • Practical task completion and quality.
  • Final project functionality, relevance and originality.
  • Documentation and submission discipline.
  • Ability to explain your work and answer questions.

Certificate eligibility

Certificate is assessment-based. Students who complete the required activities, submit the final project and pass the assessment may qualify, subject to the programme's final review. Application or attendance alone does not guarantee a certificate.

Career transparency: This is a learning internship, not a guaranteed placement programme. Employer names mentioned as examples are not claimed as partners. Job openings change frequently and students are responsible for checking current requirements.
